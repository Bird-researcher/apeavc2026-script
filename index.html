Challenges in ECG Acquisition

🎤 ENGLISH SCRIPT

However, obtaining an ECG from small birds is challenging.

In clinical practice, an arrhythmia may be suspected during auscultation, but confirmation can be difficult.

Standard ECG recording is often complicated by handling stress, the risk of anesthesia, and motion artifacts.

In particular, obtaining stable ECG waveforms from conscious small birds has been problematic.

Therefore, we needed a simple and reliable method for ECG recording during routine restraint.

🇯🇵 日本語訳

しかし、小型鳥類から心電図を取得することには困難があります。

臨床現場では、聴診によって不整脈が疑われても、それを確認することは容易ではありません。

通常の心電図記録では、保定ストレス、麻酔のリスク、体動アーチファクトなどが問題となります。

特に、意識下の小型鳥類から安定した心電図波形を取得することは困難でした。

そこで、通常の保定中に使用できる、簡単で信頼性の高い心電図記録方法が必要だと考えました。

Objectives

🎤 ENGLISH SCRIPT

Based on these challenges, we established three objectives.

First, to evaluate the feasibility of a novel coil-shaped device for ECG recording.

Second, to establish baseline Lead II ECG morphology in conscious small psittacines.

第三に、異常波形や不整脈の可視化・検出における臨床的有用性を評価することです。

Novel Coil-Shaped Device

🎤 ENGLISH SCRIPT

To overcome these limitations, we developed a novel coil-shaped electrode device.

The operator wears examination gloves, and the coil electrodes are placed directly on the index and middle fingers.

This allows the electrodes to be incorporated into the normal handling technique for small birds.

これにより、電極を小型鳥類の通常の保定手技に組み込むことができます。

ECG Analysis

🎤 ENGLISH SCRIPT

We selected the four avian species with the highest number of measurements.

For each individual, five stable Lead II waveforms were extracted.

We then calculated the mean values and analyzed the P, Q, R, S, and T wave amplitudes.

Avian Lead II has a characteristic morphology, including a prominent negative S wave.

Reference Waveforms

🎤 ENGLISH SCRIPT

Conclusion

🎤 ENGLISH SCRIPT

In conclusion, we achieved three important findings.

First, the novel coil-shaped device enabled safe and rapid ECG recording in conscious small birds without sedation or anesthesia.

Second, we established baseline ECG waveforms across four small psittacine species.

Third, we demonstrated the clinical utility of this method for visualizing and detecting avian arrhythmias.

Taken together, this technique provides a practical and reliable foundation for ECG evaluation in avian clinical practice.

第三に、この方法が鳥類の不整脈を可視化・検出する上で臨床的に有用であることを示しました。

以上から、この手法は鳥類臨床における心電図評価のための、実用的で信頼できる基盤になると考えています。

Take-Home Message

🎤 ENGLISH SCRIPT

Finally, I would like to summarize the take-home messages.

First, our novel coil-shaped device enables safe and anesthesia-free ECG recording in conscious small birds during routine handling.

Second, the reference waveforms established in four small psittacine species provide an important benchmark for identifying ECG abnormalities and arrhythmias.

Third, routine ECG recording in conscious birds may open new possibilities for earlier diagnosis and advanced cardiovascular care.

To conclude, this novel device provides a practical and non-invasive foundation for avian ECG analysis.

Thank you very much for your kind attention.

🇯🇵 日本語訳

最後に、本研究のTake-home Messageをまとめます。

第一に、新しいコイル型デバイスによって、通常の保定中に、意識下の小型鳥類から安全かつ無麻酔で心電図を記録できます。

第二に、4種類の小型インコ類で確立した基準波形は、心電図異常や不整脈を識別するための重要な基準となります。

第三に、意識下の鳥類における日常的な心電図記録は、早期診断や、より高度な心血管診療につながる新しい可能性を開くと考えています。

結論として、この新しいデバイスは、鳥類の心電図解析のための実用的かつ非侵襲的な基盤を提供します。
